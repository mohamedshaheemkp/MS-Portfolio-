Chief Creative Director's Review

This is where I want to push the project beyond even many award-winning portfolios.

Add a "Design Critique Gate"

Every significant screen should pass three questions before it is considered complete:

1. The Blur Test

Zoom out or blur the screen until text is unreadable.

Ask:

Is there still a clear visual hierarchy?
Can I identify the dominant focal point?
Does the composition still feel balanced?

If not, the hierarchy needs improvement.

2. The Black & White Test

Remove all color.

Ask:

Does the interface still work?
Is contrast carried by typography, spacing, and composition rather than accent colors?

Premium interfaces rely on structure before color.

3. The 5-Second Test

Show the screen to someone for five seconds.

Then ask:

What was this page about?
What caught your eye first?
What action would you take next?

If their answers don't match your intended hierarchy, redesign the screen.
