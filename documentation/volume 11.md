11.39 Acceptance Criteria

Volume 11 is complete when:

Every AI project follows a consistent, research-oriented structure.
Technical decisions are explained, not merely stated.
Visualizations communicate complex AI concepts clearly.
AI projects demonstrate engineering depth beyond metrics.
The experience remains accessible, performant, and understandable to both technical and non-technical audiences.
