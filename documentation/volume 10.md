Version: 1.0

10.0 Purpose

The Systems Archive is the signature experience of MS Portfolio.

This is not another Projects section.

It is not a file explorer.

It is not a gallery.

It is not a folder UI.

The Systems Archive is a Digital Knowledge System that transforms the portfolio into an engineering workspace.

If recruiters remember only one thing after leaving the portfolio, it should be this experience.

10.1 Vision

The Systems Archive should feel like entering a professional engineering studio.

Visitors should feel they have been given access to the creator's internal workspace.

Not private.

Curated.

Organized.

Thoughtful.

The experience should communicate

"This person doesn't just build projects.

They build systems."

10.43 Acceptance Criteria

Volume 10 is complete when:

The Systems Archive functions as a structured knowledge platform rather than a project gallery.
Every project can expose documentation, architecture, media, and development history in a consistent way.
Search, filtering, metadata, and relationships allow visitors to discover information naturally.
The archive remains scalable for future projects, research, writing, and documentation.
The experience reinforces the portfolio's identity as a system built by an engineer, not merely a showcase assembled by a designer.
10.44 Definition of Done (Volume 10)

Volume 10 is complete when a visitor can enter the Systems Archive and immediately understand that it is the intellectual core of the portfolio.

If someone asks:

"Where do I explore technical work?"
"How are projects organized?"
"Can I understand the architecture behind a project?"
"Can I search by technology or topic?"
"Can I see how a project evolved over time?"
"Where are the supporting documents, research, and diagrams?"

the archive already provides those answers through a coherent, discoverable experience.
