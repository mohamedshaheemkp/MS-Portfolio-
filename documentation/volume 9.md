9.45 Acceptance Criteria

Volume 9 is complete when:

Every project follows a consistent case study structure.
Technical depth is communicated through architecture, decisions, and evidence rather than claims.
AI, software, and design projects each have presentation patterns suited to their nature while remaining visually consistent.
Project pages are reusable templates driven by structured data.
Visitors can understand the problem, process, implementation, and outcome without leaving the portfolio.
Each project demonstrates engineering maturity, not just implementation.
9.46 Definition of Done (Volume 9)

Volume 9 is complete when every project in the portfolio can answer:

What problem did it solve?
Why was it built?
How was it researched?
How was it architected?
What technologies were chosen and why?
What challenges arose?
How were they solved?
What measurable outcomes were achieved?
What would be improved in a future version?
Where can the visitor explore the code or live demo?

A project page should feel complete enough that a recruiter or engineering manager could evaluate both your technical capability and your decision-making process without needing additional explanation.
